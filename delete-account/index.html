Delete Your Data

ARROUT operates locally on your device by default, with optional Google Cloud backup via Google Sign-In. You have full control to delete all local and cloud data at any time.

Delete it yourself (instant)

  1. In the game: open Settings and tap Reset Local Progress twice to confirm. This erases your name, coins, progress, themes, streaks and settings.
  2. In a browser: clear the site data for the game in your browser settings.
  3. In the Android app: clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
⚠️ This cannot be undone

Because nothing is saved online, deleted progress, coins and themes cannot be restored.

What gets deleted

  • Profile: player name and random player ID.
  • Progress: cleared levels, best times, daily streak, weekly puzzle time, battle record and achievements.
  • Coins and themes and your settings.
