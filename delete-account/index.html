Request Account & Data Deletion

Instructions and direct request method for deleting your ARROUT game account and associated personal data.

At ARROUT, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. If you have created an account or saved game progress and wish to have your account and all associated data permanently deleted, you can submit a request at any time.

One-Click Email Request

Clicking the button below will immediately open your email app with your deletion request pre-filled to our privacy desk.

✉️ Send Deletion Request Email

How the Deletion Process Works

  1. Submit Request: Click the button above or send an email to web.sannivesham@gmail.com with the subject "ARROUT - Account & Data Deletion Request".
  2. Provide Account Identifiers: Please include your in-game username/display name, registered email, or Player ID so we can verify and identify your account records.
  3. Confirmation & Deletion: We will process your request within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and all associated records have been wiped from our servers.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Profile & Authentication Data: Display name, registered email address, authentication credentials, and profile avatars.
  • Game Progression: Cleared levels, puzzle progress, stars, badges, and unlockable achievements.
  • Virtual Currency & Inventory: Virtual coin balances, unlocked arrow skins, board themes, and customized profile frames.
  • Multiplayer & Leaderboards: Match statistics, historical match logs, tournament rankings, and global leaderboard placements.

Data Retention Exceptions

Certain transaction records or security logs may be retained only for as long as required by applicable laws, tax regulations, chargeback fraud prevention, or resolving active disputes, after which they are permanently expunged.

⚠️ Irreversible Action Warning

Deleting your ARROUT account is permanent. Once completed, your progression, virtual coins, and custom skins cannot be restored or transferred to another account.
