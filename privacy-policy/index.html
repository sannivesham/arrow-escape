1. Introduction & Developer Identity

Welcome to ARROUT (“we,” “our,” or “us”). ARROUT is an arrow-based puzzle game offering single-player levels, daily challenges, weekly tournaments, online 1v1 multiplayer duels, and free virtual coin rewards.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and safeguard your information when you download, install, or play ARROUT on mobile devices (including via the Google Play Store) or access our web versions.

We are committed to handling your information transparently, ethically, and in full compliance with applicable privacy laws, including the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play Developer Program Policies (Data Safety & Families Policy), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect or process information that is genuinely necessary to operate game features, save your puzzle progress, and facilitate fair multiplayer competitions.

A. Account and Profile Information (Optional)

If you create a player profile or use account synchronization, we may collect:

  • Display Name / Username: A player-chosen nickname displayed in the game and on public leaderboards. We encourage players not to use their real full names.
  • Email Address: Only if provided for registration, account sign-in, or account recovery.
  • Authentication Identifiers: Anonymous user tokens provided by authentication providers (such as Google Play Games Services).
  • Profile Avatar: Pre-selected in-game avatar icon or custom profile thumbnail.
  • Settings & Preferences: Audio volume, haptic feedback toggles, and appearance preferences.

B. Gameplay Progress & Statistics

To enable level progression, save games, and rank online duels, we store:

  • Levels unlocked, completed, and 3-star completion scores.
  • Leaderboard rankings, tournament records, and match results.
  • Multiplayer duel statistics (MMR rating, matches won, matches played).
  • Virtual coin balance earned through gameplay achievements.

C. Device & Technical Information

To diagnose technical crashes and guarantee performance across Android device configurations, we or our trusted service providers (e.g. Google Play Services) may process:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version (e.g. Android 13).
  • Crash logs, stack traces, and system performance metrics.
  • Internet Protocol (IP) address (used solely for establishing low-latency multiplayer connections and regional server routing).
  • Anonymous app instance identifiers or advertising ID (AAID) for fraud prevention, analytics, or compliant advertising.

3. No Real-Money Purchases or Financial Data 100% Free

ARROUT does NOT offer real-money purchases, microtransactions, or paid virtual items.

  • We do NOT collect, request, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, bank account credentials, billing addresses, or payment transaction histories.
  • All levels, game modes (Classic Mode, Daily Challenge, Weekly Tournament, Online 1v1 Battle), and in-game shop items are completely accessible through regular gameplay without spending real currency.
  • If any third-party claims to sell ARROUT items or coins for real money, it is unauthorized.

4. Device Permissions (Zero Sensitive Permissions Required)

In accordance with Google Play's Permissions policy, ARROUT does not ask for or use any dangerous or sensitive runtime device permissions:

  • ❌ No Camera Access: We do not access your camera.
  • ❌ No Microphone / Audio Recording: We do not record or listen to audio.
  • ❌ No Contacts or Phonebook: We do not access your phone contacts.
  • ❌ No Precise Location (GPS): We do not track or request your precise geographical coordinates.
  • ❌ No SMS or Phone State: We do not read text messages or phone call status.
  • ❌ No Storage / Photos: We do not access your personal media or device storage.
  • ✔️ Standard Network Access: ARROUT only requests standard internet access (`android.permission.INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) to synchronize online multiplayer battles, tournaments, and cloud progress.

5. How We Use Information

We use the minimal information collected exclusively for legitimate game operations:

  • To save, maintain, and restore your level progress across game sessions.
  • To operate online 1v1 multiplayer matchmaking and calculate fair competitive leaderboard standings.
  • To award virtual coins for puzzle achievements and maintain in-game reward inventories.
  • To identify technical bugs, monitor server latency, and prevent crash regressions.
  • To detect and prevent cheating, exploits, and bot manipulation to ensure fair play for all users.
  • To respond directly to support inquiries and account deletion requests.

We do not use your personal information for automated profiling, credit scoring, or purposes unrelated to the game.

6. Online Multiplayer, Duels & Public Information

When participating in Online 1v1 Battles, Weekly Tournaments, or public Leaderboards, certain non-sensitive game profile information is visible to other players:

  • Your chosen username or display nickname.
  • Your selected avatar icon.
  • Your puzzle scores, win-loss records, and leaderboard ranking.

Security Tip: We strongly advise all players to choose a fun, creative gaming pseudonym and avoid using real names, phone numbers, or private personal details as their in-game display name.

7. Free In-Game Virtual Coins & Items

ARROUT features virtual coins and stars solely as gameplay progression rewards:

  • Virtual coins are digital game tokens earned solely by clearing puzzle boards, finishing daily puzzles, and winning friendly duels.
  • Virtual coins cannot be bought with real money, cannot be redeemed for real-world currency, and have no monetary value.
  • Virtual coins are non-transferable outside the game and exist exclusively to unlock cosmetic puzzle items and hints within the ARROUT app.

8. Advertising and Analytics

To keep ARROUT free for all players, we may display non-intrusive advertisements or utilize privacy-conscious analytics services provided by established platforms such as Google:

  • Google Play Services: Core backend services for game authentication, cloud saves, and achievement reporting. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: May serve banner or interstitial ads in compliant formats. AdMob may process your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID) in accordance with Google's policies.

Your Opt-Out Choices: You can manage or disable personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by visiting: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

9. How We Share Information

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information for monetary or commercial compensation.

Information may only be shared under the following strictly limited circumstances:

  • Trusted Technical Service Providers: Cloud hosting, database infrastructure, and crash diagnostic providers bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Legal and Safety Requirements: If required by valid court order, applicable law, or to protect the safety, security, and integrity of our players and services.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or restructuring, where user data is transferred under terms consistent with this Privacy Policy.

10. Data Security

We implement robust technical and administrative safeguards to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the game client and backend servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS cryptographic protocols.
  • Access Controls: Server infrastructure and databases are restricted to authorized personnel with strict multi-factor authentication.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Regular reviews of security practices to guard against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of data.

11. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

We retain gameplay and profile data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide game services.
