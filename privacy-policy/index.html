1. Introduction & Developer Identity

Welcome to ARROUT (“we,” “our,” or “us”). ARROUT is a free arrow-escape puzzle game with 1,000 campaign levels, a daily challenge, a weekly puzzle, battles against a computer opponent, and rooms for playing with friends.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game stores and who can see it. It covers the web version and the app version of ARROUT.

Developer: reshwanth

Contact Email: web.sannivesham@gmail.com

Official Website: https://sannivesham.github.io/arrow-escape/

2. Information We Collect

We do not run a server that collects your data. ARROUT has no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud save. Everything below is stored only on your own device, in the browser or app storage, and is never sent to us.

  • Player name and random player ID: a nickname you can change in Profile. Please do not use your real full name.
  • Progress: cleared levels, best times, daily-challenge streak, weekly-puzzle best time, battle record and rating, achievements.
  • Virtual coins and unlocked themes.
  • Settings: sound, volume, vibration, arrow speed, display preferences.

We do not collect your email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID.

3. No Payments or Financial Data 100% Free

ARROUT does NOT offer real-money purchases. Coins are a free in-game reward and cannot be bought or cashed out. We do not collect any payment or financial information.

4. Device Permissions

ARROUT does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, phone or storage-access permissions. It only needs normal internet access so the page can load its fonts. Vibration is used for game feedback if you leave it switched on.

5. How We Use Information

The data stored on your device is used only to save your progress, show your stats and achievements, and apply your settings. We do not use it for profiling or advertising.

6. Battles, Rooms & Public Information

  • Battle: your opponent is controlled by the game on your device. No other player is involved and nothing about the match is sent anywhere.
  • Rooms (play with friends): in this version a room only connects tabs or windows of the same browser on the same device. Your nickname is shared with those tabs only.
  • Leaderboards: there are no public leaderboards. Your best times are shown only to you.

If we add real online play in a future version, we will update this policy before it is released.

7. Free In-Game Virtual Coins

Coins are earned by clearing levels, daily challenges and achievements. They have no real-world value. Because progress is kept on your device, clearing your browser data, resetting progress or uninstalling the app removes your coins.

8. Advertising, Analytics & Third Parties

  • No ads and no analytics. ARROUT does not show ads, track you across websites, or sell your data.
  • Google Firebase: When you use Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore process authentication tokens and store your game saves securely under strict user-only security rules. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: the game loads its fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Like any website that does this, your IP address and browser details are sent to Google to deliver the fonts. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting: the web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) under GitHub’s privacy statement.

9. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not receive any. The only third parties that can see technical data such as your IP address are the font and hosting providers named in section 8.

10. Data Security

The game is served over HTTPS. Your progress lives in your own device storage, so its security depends on your device. Anyone with access to your device or browser tools can view or change this data.

11. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep nothing on our side. To delete your data yourself, open Settings → Reset Local Progress in the game, clear the site data for the game in your browser, or uninstall the app. See the data deletion page. You can also email us with any question.

12. Children's Privacy

ARROUT collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no ads, chat or purchases. The nickname field is free text that stays on the device, and we ask children not to enter their real name.

13. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You control all stored data directly as described in section 11. If you believe we hold information about you, email us and we will respond.

14. Changes to This Policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your data (for example, adding online play, accounts, ads or analytics), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change is released.
